# Explore Canada Bay

Explore Canada Bay is a Flutter application being developed for a NSW Year 11 Design & Technology Major Project.

The aim is to encourage residents and visitors to explore the City of Canada Bay through walking routes, parks, biodiversity and local attractions.

The app should feel modern, premium and welcoming while remaining simple to use.

## Primary Features

- Interactive OpenStreetMap
- Walking routes loaded from GPX files
- Route recommendations
- Parks
- Outdoor gyms
- Biodiversity locations
- Food & cafés
- Attractions
- Community facilities
- XP system
- Achievements
- Profile
- Digital passport
- QR checkpoint rewards
