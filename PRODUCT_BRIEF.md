# Explore Canada Bay
## Product Brief

---

# Product Vision

Explore Canada Bay is a location-based civic and community discovery platform that helps people who are new to the City of Canada Bay become confident members of their local community.

Rather than acting as a tourism app or fitness tracker, the application combines civic information, community engagement, environmental education and local exploration into a single platform.

The overall goal is to reduce the barriers experienced by people unfamiliar with the area by making it easier to discover local services, recreational opportunities, community organisations, businesses and natural environments.

---

# Problem Statement

Moving into a new area can be overwhelming.

New residents often do not know:

- where local parks and walking tracks are
- how local council services operate
- where community events are held
- how to become involved in local organisations
- what local businesses are nearby
- what environmental assets exist around them

This information already exists, however it is spread across multiple council websites, organisations and community groups.

Explore Canada Bay centralises this information into one modern and accessible application.

---

# Target Market

The application is designed for people who are **new to the City of Canada Bay**.

Examples include:

- residents moving from another Sydney suburb
- interstate relocations
- international migrants
- university students
- young professionals
- families
- retirees

The application is **not** exclusively designed for migrants.

Anyone unfamiliar with the local area should benefit.

---

# Product Goals

The application should help users:

- integrate into their local community
- become familiar with the City of Canada Bay
- discover parks, routes and attractions
- understand local council services
- connect with community organisations
- participate in local events
- support local businesses
- develop environmental awareness
- encourage healthy outdoor recreation

---

# Core Principles

Every feature must support one or more of the following objectives.

### Community

Strengthen community participation and belonging.

### Civic Information

Improve access to council services and practical information.

### Exploration

Encourage users to discover new places.

### Environment

Promote appreciation of local biodiversity and sustainability.

### Health

Encourage active lifestyles through walking and cycling.

### Local Economy

Support local cafés, restaurants and businesses.

---

# Core Features

## Home

The Home page should provide:

- personalised recommendations
- nearby places
- featured route
- featured community event
- featured environmental discovery
- quick access to important civic information

---

## Explore

Discover:

- parks
- walking routes
- cycling routes
- attractions
- biodiversity sites
- cafés
- restaurants
- community facilities
- libraries
- local businesses

---

## Community

Provide curated information about:

- council events
- library programs
- sporting clubs
- walking groups
- cycling groups
- volunteer organisations
- bushcare
- festivals
- markets
- community organisations

This is **not** a social media platform.

---

## Local Services

Include practical civic information such as:

- bin collection
- recycling centres
- council BBQs
- libraries
- public transport
- parking
- public toilets
- emergency information
- when to call 000
- reporting council issues
- dog parks

Always direct users to official sources where appropriate.

---

## Passport

Retain the Community Passport.

Instead of acting purely as gamification, it should encourage users to:

- visit parks
- attend events
- support local businesses
- complete walking routes
- discover biodiversity
- visit libraries
- explore heritage locations

The passport should celebrate exploration rather than competition.

---

## QR Scanner

Retain the QR Scanner.

QR codes may appear at:

- parks
- environmental sites
- heritage locations
- public art
- libraries
- council facilities
- community events

Scanning QR codes should unlock information, passport stamps and educational content.

---

# Environmental Awareness

Environmental education is a major feature.

The application should educate users about:

- Powells Creek
- Parramatta River
- native birds
- native plants
- biodiversity
- habitat restoration
- rewilding
- sustainability
- environmental volunteering

All environmental information should be connected to real places.

---

# Language Accessibility

Support multilingual users through Flutter localisation.

Initially prepare the application for:

- English
- Simplified Chinese
- Korean
- Italian
- Hindi

Language support should improve accessibility while keeping official place names unchanged.

# Success Criteria

The project is successful if a new resident can:

- understand the local area
- access civic information
- discover parks and attractions
- join community activities
- learn about the environment
- support local businesses
- feel more connected to the City of Canada Bay
