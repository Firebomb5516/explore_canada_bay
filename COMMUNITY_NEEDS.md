# Explore Canada Bay – Community Need & Design Justification

## Design Opportunity

The City of Canada Bay is one of Sydney's fastest-growing and most culturally diverse local government areas. Every year, people move into the area from elsewhere in Sydney, interstate and overseas. While the City offers an extensive range of parks, walking and cycling routes, community organisations, local businesses and environmental assets, this information is often dispersed across numerous council webpages, community organisations and third-party websites.

For residents who are new to the area, locating and understanding these opportunities can be time-consuming and overwhelming.

**Explore Canada Bay** aims to reduce this information barrier by bringing trusted local information into a single, location-based platform that encourages community participation, active lifestyles and environmental awareness.

---

# Target Market

The primary target market is **people who are new to the City of Canada Bay**, including:

- residents relocating from another Sydney suburb
- people moving from interstate
- international migrants
- university students
- young professionals
- families
- retirees

The app is designed for **anyone unfamiliar with the local area**, not exclusively migrants.

---

# Evidence of Community Need

The design is based on priorities identified by the City of Canada Bay through community consultation.

## 1. Building Community Connections

The City of Canada Bay's **Community Strategic Plan – Our Future 2036** identifies **"Connected Community"** as one of its five strategic directions. The plan recognises that strong communities are created through participation, inclusion and opportunities for residents to connect with one another. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

For someone new to the area, these opportunities can be difficult to discover because community groups, volunteering programs, sporting clubs and local events are promoted across many different platforms.

### Design Response

The app will include:

- Community Hub
- local events
- sporting clubs
- volunteering opportunities
- library programs
- council activities
- community organisations

These features help residents become active members of their new community.

---

## 2. Encouraging Active Living

Community consultation identified recreation facilities, walking, cycling and access to open space as important priorities for residents. Improving infrastructure that supports walking and cycling also forms part of Council's long-term vision. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

People moving into the area may simply not know where these opportunities exist.

### Design Response

The app provides:

- walking routes
- cycling routes
- parks
- playgrounds
- outdoor gyms
- waterfront reserves
- route recommendations
- accessibility information

This encourages exploration and healthier lifestyles.

---

## 3. Improving Access to Local Services

New residents often require practical information before they can confidently participate in everyday community life.

Rather than searching multiple websites, users should be able to quickly locate civic information relevant to living in the City of Canada Bay.

### Design Response

The app will include:

- bin collection information
- recycling centres
- council BBQ locations
- public transport
- parking information
- libraries
- community centres
- emergency contacts (000)
- council reporting services

This transforms the app into a practical civic guide rather than simply a map.

---

## 4. Environmental Awareness

The Community Strategic Plan identifies a **Sustainable and Thriving Environment** as another major direction for the future of the City of Canada Bay. Community consultation highlighted biodiversity, waterways, waste reduction, recycling, tree canopy and protection of natural environments as important priorities. :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}

People who are unfamiliar with the area may not realise the ecological significance of locations such as Powells Creek, the Parramatta River or local habitat restoration projects.

### Design Response

The app will include:

- biodiversity waypoints
- native bird information
- native plant information
- Powells Creek restoration
- Parramatta River ecology
- rewilding projects
- sustainability tips
- environmental volunteering opportunities

Environmental information will always be linked to real locations so users can learn while exploring.

---

## 5. Supporting Local Businesses

Discovering local cafés, restaurants and businesses helps new residents become familiar with neighbourhood centres while also supporting the local economy.

### Design Response

The app will recommend:

- cafés
- restaurants
- local businesses
- markets
- shopping precincts

Recommendations will be location-based and integrated with nearby parks, attractions and walking routes.

---

# Design Objectives

Explore Canada Bay should help users:

- integrate into the local community
- discover community organisations
- access local services
- participate in community life
- become more physically active
- support local businesses
- develop environmental awareness
- build confidence living in the City of Canada Bay

---

# Feature Evaluation Criteria

Every feature should satisfy at least one of the following objectives:

- Helps a new resident settle into the area.
- Strengthens community participation.
- Improves access to local services.
- Encourages active recreation.
- Promotes environmental awareness.
- Supports the local economy.

If a feature does not contribute to one of these objectives, it should be reconsidered.

---

# Final Product Statement

**Explore Canada Bay** is a location-based civic and community discovery platform designed to help people who are new to the City of Canada Bay integrate into their local community by making it easier to discover local services, recreational opportunities, community organisations, local businesses and the area's natural environment.

By combining civic information, recreation, environmental education and community engagement into one application, the project directly responds to priorities identified by the City of Canada Bay while addressing the practical challenges faced by residents who are unfamiliar with the area.
